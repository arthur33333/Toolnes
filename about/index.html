About Toolnes

What is Toolnes?

Toolnes is a collection of free online tools built to help you get everyday tasks done quickly. You will find calculators, converters, text tools, developer helpers, PDF and image utilities, finance calculators and puzzles, all in one place.

Tools for authors and self-publishers

Our KDP & Publishing section helps authors plan books for Amazon KDP. You can estimate royalties and profit margins, convert word count into pages, check trim sizes, calculate spine width and organize your launch.

Our approach

  • Free to use. No account or sign-up is needed.
  • Simple and fast. Tools load quickly and work on phones and computers.
  • Private by design. Most calculations run in your browser, so what you type stays on your device.
  • Honest estimates. Our planning tools give estimates only. Always verify final figures, such as KDP printing costs and royalties, with the official source.
