How to use the Scientific Calculator

Choose degrees or radians for trigonometric calculations. Use sin, cos and tan for trigonometric functions; log for base-10 logarithms; ln for natural logarithms; and x² or xʸ for powers. Use parentheses to group expressions. For example, sin(30) in degree mode returns 0.5.
