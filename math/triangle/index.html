Triangle Calculator

Calculate a triangle's area and perimeter, or find a missing side of a right triangle.

Enter all three side lengths. The calculator checks whether they form a valid triangle.
For the hypotenuse, enter the two shorter sides. For a missing shorter side, enter the hypotenuse first, then the known shorter side.

Results

Triangle formulas

Perimeter = a + b + c
Area (Heron's formula) = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]

Here, s is the semiperimeter: (a + b + c) ÷ 2.

Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c²

For a right triangle, c is the hypotenuse, the side opposite the right angle.

How to use this calculator

  • Select whether you want the area and perimeter or a missing side.
  • Enter positive side lengths and choose the unit.
  • Click Calculate Triangle to see the results.
  • Use Load Example to try sample values, or Reset to start again.
