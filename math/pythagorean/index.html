The Pythagorean theorem

This theorem applies only to right triangles, which have one angle equal to 90 degrees.

a² + b² = c²

c is the hypotenuse, the longest side. a and b are the two shorter sides.

Hypotenuse: c = √(a² + b²)
Missing side: a = √(c² − b²)
Missing side: b = √(c² − a²)
