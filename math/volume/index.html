Volume Calculator

Calculate the volume of common three-dimensional shapes.

Volume formulas

Cube
V = side³
Rectangular prism
V = length × width × height
Cylinder
V = π × radius² × height
Sphere
V = ⁴⁄₃ × π × radius³
Cone
V = ⅓ × π × radius² × height
Square pyramid
V = ⅓ × base side² × height

Enter all measurements in the same unit. The volume will be shown in cubic units, such as cm³ or m³.
