What is a factorial?

The factorial of a non-negative integer n, written as n!, is the product of all positive integers from 1 to n.

For example, 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120.

By definition, 0! = 1.

How is the factorial calculated?

The calculator multiplies each whole number from n down to 1. It supports values from 0 to 170, which can be represented as finite numbers in standard JavaScript numeric arithmetic.

n! = n × (n − 1) × (n − 2) × … × 2 × 1
