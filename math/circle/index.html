Circle formulas

A circle is a two-dimensional shape where every point on its edge is the same distance from the center.

Diameter = 2 × Radius
Circumference = 2 × π × Radius
Area = π × Radius²

Here, π (pi) is approximately 3.141592653589793.

How to use this calculator

  • Select the measurement you already know.
  • Enter its positive value and choose the unit.
  • Click Calculate Circle to display the four measurements.
  • Use Reset to start a new calculation.

For area, the result is expressed in square units, such as cm² or m². Other results use the selected linear unit.
