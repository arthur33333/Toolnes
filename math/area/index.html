Area Calculator

Calculate the area of common 2D geometric shapes in seconds.

Area formulas

Rectangle
Length × width
Square
Side × side
Triangle
½ × base × height
Parallelogram
Base × height
Trapezoid
½ × (base 1 + base 2) × height
Circle
π × radius²
Ellipse
π × radius 1 × radius 2

Enter all measurements in the same unit. The resulting area will be expressed in square units, such as cm² or m².
