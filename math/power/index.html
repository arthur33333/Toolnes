What is a power?

A power represents repeated multiplication. In aⁿ, the number a is the base and n is the exponent.

2⁵ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32

A zero exponent gives 1 for any non-zero base. A negative exponent represents the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power.

a⁰ = 1 (when a ≠ 0)   |   a⁻ⁿ = 1 / aⁿ
