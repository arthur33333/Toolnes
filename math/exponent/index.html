What is an exponent?

An exponent tells you how many times a base is multiplied by itself when the exponent is a positive whole number.

2⁵ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32

To solve aˣ = b for x when a and b are positive and a ≠ 1, use logarithms:

x = log(b) / log(a)
