What is a square root?

The square root of a number is a value that, when multiplied by itself, gives the original number.

For example, the square root of 25 is 5 because 5 × 5 = 25.

If √x = y, then y × y = x.

Perfect squares

Perfect squares have whole-number square roots. Examples include:

  • √1 = 1
  • √4 = 2
  • √9 = 3
  • √16 = 4
  • √25 = 5
  • √100 = 10

Other positive numbers can have decimal square roots. For example, √2 is approximately 1.41421356.
