How to use the stopwatch

  1. Click Start to begin measuring elapsed time.
  2. Click Lap to record a split time without stopping the timer.
  3. Click Pause to temporarily stop the timer, then click Resume to continue.
  4. Click Reset to return the stopwatch to zero.
  5. Use Download CSV to save your recorded lap times.

Accurate elapsed-time measurement

The stopwatch uses the browser's high-resolution performance timer rather than counting fixed intervals. This helps keep elapsed time accurate even when the browser temporarily slows down screen updates.
