Convert a date and time between cities and time zones around the world.

How to Use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Select the original time zone and the destination time zone.
  2. Choose the date and time you want to convert.
  3. Click Convert Time to see the local date and time at the destination.

Why Can the Time Difference Change?

Some locations observe daylight saving time, while others do not. This converter uses the time zone rules built into your browser to account for date-dependent differences.

Time zones are selected by region and city, rather than by a fixed UTC offset, so seasonal changes can be reflected.
