Crossword Generator

Create a small crossword-style word puzzle from your own words and clues.

1. Enter your words and clues

Enter one item per line in this format: WORD - Clue.

Use letters and short words for the best results. Example: CAT - A small pet.

How to use this generator

  1. Enter a word and its clue on each line.
  2. Click Generate Puzzle.
  3. Use the clues to solve the puzzle.
  4. Print the puzzle if you want to solve it on paper.
