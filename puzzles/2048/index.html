Use your keyboard arrow keys, swipe on the board, or use the buttons below. Matching tiles merge into one larger tile.

How to Play 2048

1. Move the numbered tiles up, down, left, or right.

2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge.

3. Each merge adds the new tile's value to your score.

4. Reach the 2048 tile to win. You can continue playing afterward!
