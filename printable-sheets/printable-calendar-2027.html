About this 2027 calendar

The yearly page shows all twelve months together, which is ideal for planning holidays, school terms and big projects. The monthly pages have large boxes for appointments, birthdays and reminders. There are no holidays printed on the pages, because they differ from country to country.

  • Pick Monday as the first day if your week starts on Monday.
  • Add a title such as a family name, a project or a school class.
  • Print the year page and keep it where you plan most often.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 2027 calendar free to print?

Yes. You can download or print the year calendar and any monthly page for free for personal, home, classroom and small office use.

Can I choose Sunday or Monday as the first day of the week?

Yes. Use the Week starts on option to switch. Every page updates instantly.

Does it work with A4 paper?

Yes. Choose A4 or US Letter under Paper size before you download or print.

Does the calendar include holidays?

No. Holidays differ by country, so the calendar is left clean and you can write your own dates in the large boxes.
