How to print

  1. Click any page to open it in large size.
  2. Press Print, choose US Letter or A4 and set scale to 100%.
  3. Or press Download PNG to save a high-quality image.
  4. Use Copy link to share a page or find it again later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these coloring pages free?

Yes. You can download and print any page for free for personal, home and classroom use.

How many coloring pages are there?

Every page is created from a unique number, so there are thousands of different designs in each category, and a new set appears every day under "New today".

How do I print a coloring page?

Open a page, press Print and choose US Letter or A4 paper at 100% scale. You can also download the PNG file.

Can I find the same page again later?

Yes. Every page has a number and its own link. Use Copy link to save or share it, or switch to the Classic library, where page numbers always stay the same.
