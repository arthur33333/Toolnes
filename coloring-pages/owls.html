Owl Coloring Pages

Owl coloring pages combine a cozy night-time scene with big, expressive eyes. Each owl sits on a branch under stars and sometimes a crescent moon, and its wings and feathered belly offer lots of small shapes to fill. Click any page to download or print it for free.

Ideas for coloring your Owl pages

  • Try a dark blue sky with yellow stars for a night-time mood.
  • Use warm browns and creams on the body, and bright colors for the eyes.
  • Color each belly feather a slightly different shade.

Every page is a unique design created from its own number. Use Copy link in the large view to save a favorite page and find it again later.

Frequently Asked Questions

What colors are best for an owl?

Natural browns, grays and creams look realistic, but fantasy owls in purple, teal or pink are just as fun.

Do owl pages work for bedtime or Halloween themes?

Yes. The moon and stars suit a bedtime activity, and an owl in dark colors makes a great autumn or Halloween page.

Are these coloring pages free?

Yes. You can download and print any page for free for personal, home and classroom use.

How do I print a page?

Open a page, press Print, choose US Letter or A4 and set scale to 100%. You can also download the PNG file.
