Why convert an image to JPG?

JPG is widely supported by websites, apps, email services and image viewers. It can also produce smaller files for photographs, depending on the image and quality setting.

Important: JPG does not support transparency

Transparent areas in PNG or other images are filled with white when converted to JPG. JPG is a lossy format, so lower quality settings can reduce file size but may also reduce image detail.
