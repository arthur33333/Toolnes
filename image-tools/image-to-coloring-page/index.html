How to make a good coloring page

  1. Choose a clear photo with a simple subject and a plain background.
  2. Upload it, then lower Detail for more fine lines or raise it for simpler shapes.
  3. Use Line sensitivity to add or remove lines, and Line thickness to make them easier to color.
  4. Download the PNG, or choose Print and select "Save as PDF".

Looking for ready-made pages? Try our free printable coloring pages. Planning a coloring book? See the KDP Cover Size Calculator.

Respect copyright: use only images you own or have permission to use. Do not turn copyrighted characters, artwork or other people's photos into pages you share or sell.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn a photo into a coloring page?

Upload your image, adjust the sliders until the outlines look right, then download the PNG or print it.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, so your image never leaves your device.

What kind of photo works best?

Clear photos with a simple subject, good lighting and a plain background give the cleanest results.

Can I sell coloring pages made with this tool?

Only if you own the rights to the original image. Never use copyrighted characters or other people's work without permission.
