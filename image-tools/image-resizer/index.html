How to Resize an Image

  1. Upload an image from your device.
  2. Enter your desired width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep “Keep aspect ratio” enabled to avoid stretching the image.
  4. Choose an output format if needed.
  5. Click “Resize Image” and download the result.

What is aspect ratio?

Aspect ratio describes the relationship between an image's width and height. Keeping it enabled helps prevent your image from looking stretched or squashed.

Is my image uploaded?

No. This tool uses your browser to resize the selected image locally. The image is not sent to a Toolnes server by this page.
