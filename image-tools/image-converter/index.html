Which image format should you choose?

WebP

WebP often creates smaller files while maintaining good visual quality. It is a useful choice for websites.

JPG

JPG is widely supported and works well for photographs. It does not preserve transparent backgrounds.

PNG

PNG is useful for graphics and transparent backgrounds. Its files can be larger than JPG or WebP.
