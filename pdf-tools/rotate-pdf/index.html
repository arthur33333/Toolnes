Rotate PDF Pages

Rotate every page in your PDF by 90°, 180°, or 270°. Download the corrected document for free.

This operation rotates all pages in the document by the selected angle. It does not rotate individual pages separately.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Select your PDF file.
  2. Choose the desired rotation angle.
  3. Click Rotate PDF.
  4. Download your rotated PDF.

Private PDF rotation

Your file is processed in your browser. The original document is not modified. Check the downloaded PDF before sharing or printing it.
