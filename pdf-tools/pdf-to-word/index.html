PDF to Word Converter

Extract text from a PDF and convert it into an editable Word document. Free to use, with processing performed in your browser.

This version works best with PDFs that contain selectable text. Scanned documents may require OCR and are not supported here.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Select a PDF file from your device.
  2. Click Convert to Word.
  3. Wait while the text is extracted.
  4. Download the editable DOCX document.

Important information

This tool extracts selectable text and places it into a Word document. Complex layouts, images, columns, and tables may not be preserved. Password-protected PDFs and scanned documents may not work.
