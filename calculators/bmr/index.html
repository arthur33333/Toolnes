How BMR Is Calculated

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the number of calories your body needs each day to maintain basic functions while at complete rest.

Toolnes uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

Men: BMR = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5

Women: BMR = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161

W = weight in kilograms, H = height in centimeters, and A = age in years.

The daily calorie figures are estimates based on activity multipliers and are not medical advice.
