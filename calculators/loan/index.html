How the Loan Calculator Works

This calculator estimates the regular payment required to repay a fixed-rate loan over a specified period.

The calculation uses the standard amortizing loan formula:

Payment = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P is the loan amount, r is the interest rate per payment period, and n is the total number of payments.

If the interest rate is 0%, the calculator simply divides the loan amount by the number of payments.
