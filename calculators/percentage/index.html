How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. Toolnes makes common percentage calculations quick and easy.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

For example, if you want to know what 20% of 150 is, multiply 150 by 20 and divide by 100.

20% of 150 = 30
