📚 How the Tax Calculator Works

Tax Amount: Price × Tax Rate ÷ 100

Price Including Tax: Price + Tax Amount

Price Before Tax: Final Price ÷ (1 + Tax Rate ÷ 100)

Example: A $100 item with a 20% tax has a $20 tax and costs $120 after tax.
