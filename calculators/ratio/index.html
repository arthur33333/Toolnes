How does a ratio work?

A ratio compares two quantities. For example, 20:30 can be simplified to 2:3.

To simplify a ratio, both numbers are divided by their greatest common divisor (GCD).

Example: 20 ÷ 10 : 30 ÷ 10 = 2:3
