How BMI Is Calculated

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a simple measurement based on weight and height.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

BMI categories shown here are commonly used adult reference ranges. BMI is a screening measurement and does not directly measure body fat or overall health.
