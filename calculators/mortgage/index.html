How the Mortgage Calculator Works

The mortgage calculator first subtracts the down payment from the home price to determine the amount borrowed.

It then calculates the monthly payment using the standard fixed-rate mortgage formula.

Payment = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P = mortgage principal, r = monthly interest rate, and n = total number of monthly payments.

This calculator does not include property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, or other additional housing costs.
