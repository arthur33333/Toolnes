How the Calorie Calculator Works

The calculator estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and then adjusts it according to your activity level.

Daily Calories = BMR × Activity Factor

The weight-loss estimate uses approximately 500 fewer calories per day, while the weight-gain estimate uses approximately 500 additional calories per day.

These are general estimates and individual calorie needs can vary considerably.
