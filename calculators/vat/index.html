📚 How VAT Calculation Works

VAT Amount: Price × VAT Rate ÷ 100

Price Including VAT: Price + VAT Amount

Price Excluding VAT: Price Including VAT ÷ (1 + VAT Rate ÷ 100)

Example: A €100 product with 20% VAT has €20 VAT and costs €120 including VAT.
