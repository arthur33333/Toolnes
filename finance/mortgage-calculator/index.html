How does a mortgage calculator work?

A mortgage calculator estimates the regular payment required to repay a home loan over a selected term. The principal is the amount borrowed after subtracting the down payment from the property price.

The interest rate and loan term affect the payment and the total interest paid. Property taxes, insurance, and other fees can increase the monthly housing cost.

This tool provides estimates only and is not a lender offer or financial advice.
