Tax is calculated after the discount. Actual tax rules may vary by location and product.

How to calculate a discount

Discount amount = Original price × Discount percentage ÷ 100.

Sale price = Original price − Discount amount.

Final price after tax = Sale price × (1 + Tax rate ÷ 100).

For example, a 20% discount on a $100 item saves $20, leaving a sale price of $80 before any tax.
