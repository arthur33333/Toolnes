How does inflation affect money?

Inflation means that prices generally rise over time. When prices increase, the same amount of money buys fewer goods and services.

Future cost = Today's amount × (1 + inflation rate)years

Purchasing power = Future amount ÷ (1 + inflation rate)years

For example, with 3% annual inflation, something that costs $1,000 today would cost approximately $1,343.92 in 10 years if that rate stayed constant.

This calculator is an estimate, not a forecast of actual inflation.
