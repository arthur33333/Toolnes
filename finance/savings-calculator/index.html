How does a savings calculator work?

A savings calculator estimates the future value of an initial balance plus regular deposits. When interest is earned, it can also generate additional interest over time. This is known as compound interest.

Your actual result may vary depending on your bank's interest calculation method, fees, taxes, and whether the rate changes.

This calculator provides an estimate and does not guarantee future returns.
