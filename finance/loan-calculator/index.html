How is a loan payment calculated?

For a fixed-rate loan with regular payments, the payment is calculated from the loan principal, periodic interest rate, and number of payments.

When the interest rate is zero, each payment is the loan amount divided by the number of payments. For loans with interest, part of each payment covers interest and the remainder reduces the balance.
