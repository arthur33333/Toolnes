What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on both your original balance and the interest accumulated over time. When interest is reinvested, growth can accelerate as the balance increases.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your starting investment and regular contributions.
  2. Choose how often you contribute and how often interest compounds.
  3. Enter an annual interest rate and the investment period.
  4. Click Calculate Growth to view the estimated future value.
