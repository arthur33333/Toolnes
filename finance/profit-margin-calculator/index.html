Understand your results

Enter your cost and selling price to calculate your profit.

Profit = Selling price − Cost

Profit margin = Profit ÷ Selling price × 100

Markup = Profit ÷ Cost × 100

The calculation does not include taxes, platform fees, shipping, advertising, or other operating expenses unless you include them in the cost.

Profit margin vs. markup

Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of revenue. Markup measures profit as a percentage of cost. They are different measurements, even when calculated from the same sale.

For example, if an item costs $60 and sells for $100, the profit is $40, the profit margin is 40%, and the markup is approximately 66.67%.
