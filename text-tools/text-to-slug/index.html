Text to Slug Converter

Turn titles, headings, and text into clean, lowercase URL slugs. Useful for creating readable website links and SEO-friendly URLs.

Spaces and punctuation are converted into hyphens. Accented Latin letters are normalized where possible.

Example
Input: My New Article About Technology!
Output: my-new-article-about-technology
