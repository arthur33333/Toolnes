How to plan your journal interior

  1. Choose a journal template as a starting point.
  2. Enter the number of pages for each interior style.
  3. Decide whether you need to add blank pages to reach a particular total.
  4. Calculate and save the estimate for your publishing plan.
  5. After designing your interior, export the PDF and check its actual page count.

The cover is not included in this calculation. The actual print file may require different pagination based on your final layout and publishing platform requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Does the total include the cover?

No. The result estimates interior pages only. The cover is prepared as a separate file for paperback publishing.

Can I mix lined, blank, and dotted pages?

Yes. Enter the number of pages for each type. The calculator adds the sections to estimate your total interior length.

Is this an official Amazon KDP tool?

No. Toolnes is an independent planning tool and does not validate or upload your files. Always check the latest KDP requirements before publishing.

Will this tell me whether my journal will be accepted?

No. It estimates page count only. Acceptance depends on the complete file and the platform's current technical and content requirements.
