What is trim size?

Trim size is the final width and height of a printed book after its pages have been cut to size. It affects the layout of your interior, the number of words that fit on a page, and the dimensions of your cover file.

Important before publishing

  • Confirm that your selected size is supported for your book's format and marketplace.
  • Set up the interior PDF using the correct page dimensions and margins.
  • Calculate the full cover separately; its dimensions also depend on spine width, bleed, and page count.

This calculator provides dimension conversions and planning estimates. It does not replace Amazon KDP's current print specifications.
