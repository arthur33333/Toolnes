How This Converter Works

The tool measures the printable area of one page (trim size minus margins), then estimates how many characters fit on each line and how many lines fit on the page based on your font size and line spacing. Dividing the word count by the words per page gives the text pages. Extra pages are added, and the total is rounded up to an even number, because paperback pages are printed on both sides of each sheet.

Tips to Control Your Page Count

  • Fiction usually reads well at 11 to 12 pt with line spacing around 1.2 to 1.4.
  • Larger trim sizes hold more words, which lowers page count and printing cost.
  • Keep inside margins large enough for the gutter. KDP requires bigger inside margins as page count grows.
  • Once you know your pages, use the spine width calculator to plan your cover.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words fit on a 6x9 book page?

With 12 pt type, 0.75 inch margins and normal line spacing, a 6x9 page holds roughly 300 words. Smaller type or tighter spacing fits more.

What is the minimum page count for a KDP paperback?

KDP paperbacks need at least 24 pages. Page counts are always even because each sheet has two sides.

Is this result exact?

No. Headings, chapter breaks, images and your real font change the final count, so check your formatted file.
