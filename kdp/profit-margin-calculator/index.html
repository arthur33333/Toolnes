How Profit Margin Is Calculated

Profit margin tells you what share of the list price you actually keep after every cost. This calculator uses:

Profit per sale = (list price × royalty rate) − printing cost − ad cost per sale
Margin = profit per sale ÷ list price

For example, with a 12.99 list price, a 60% rate, 4.20 printing and 1.50 ad cost, the royalty before costs is 7.79 and the profit is about 2.09 per sale, a margin of roughly 16%. If your target is 30%, the tool shows the higher price you would need.

Ways to improve your margin

  • Raise your price in small steps and watch your sales.
  • Lower printing cost with a smaller page count or a larger trim size.
  • Improve your ads, because a lower ad cost per sale lifts profit directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good profit margin for a KDP book?

Many authors aim for at least 25 to 30 percent after printing and advertising costs, but the right target depends on your niche and strategy.

How do I calculate profit per sale?

Multiply the list price by the royalty rate, then subtract the printing cost and any advertising cost per sale.

Why include advertising cost per sale?

If you run ads, the money spent to get each sale reduces your real profit. Including it shows whether your ads are profitable.
