How Much Does KDP Pay Per Book?

Updated October 2026 · 5 min read

The short answer

It depends on the format. Amazon KDP pays up to 60% of the list price on paperbacks and hardcovers, after subtracting the cost of printing each copy. On ebooks it pays either 35% or 70%, depending on your price and the plan you choose. The honest answer for most authors is a few dollars per paperback sale and a bit more per ebook sale at the same price.

Paperback and hardcover royalties

For print books, KDP calculates your royalty like this:

Royalty = (list price × royalty rate) − printing cost

The printing cost depends on your page count, trim size, marketplace and whether the interior is black & white or color. KDP gives this example for a 300-page black & white paperback in a regular trim size on Amazon.com: a fixed cost of $1.00 plus $0.012 per page, which comes to $4.60.

Worked example

ItemAmount
List price$14.99
Royalty before printing (60%)$8.99
Printing cost (300 pages)−$4.60
You earn per saleabout $4.39

KDP also sets a minimum list price so you cannot sell below your printing cost. You can estimate it by dividing the printing cost by the royalty rate. With a $4.60 printing cost and a 60% rate, the floor is roughly $7.67.

Expanded Distribution pays less

Books sold through Expanded Distribution (bookstores, libraries and other retailers) earn a lower rate of 40% of the list price, minus the same printing cost. Many authors treat it as a bonus rather than their main source of income.

A note on price tiers

Several publishing guides report that, since mid-2025, paperbacks priced below $9.99 earn a 50% rate instead of 60%. KDP's own page still describes print royalties as "up to 60%", so check the rate shown in your KDP pricing screen for your exact price and marketplace.

Ebook royalties

Kindle ebooks have two royalty plans:

  • 70% plan: 70% of the list price, minus a delivery fee based on file size, in an eligible price range (commonly $2.99 to $9.99) and territories.
  • 35% plan: 35% of the list price, with no delivery fee, for any price outside the 70% range.

70% plan: royalty = 70% × (list price − delivery cost)

For example, a $4.99 ebook with a 1 MB file on the 70% plan earns about $3.34 per sale, using KDP's delivery fee of $0.15 per MB. Text-only books have small files, so the fee is usually minor. Large image-heavy ebooks can lose much more.

When does KDP pay you?

KDP pays royalties monthly, with a delay of roughly 60 days after the month in which the sale happened. You need to reach the payment threshold for your payment method, so your first deposit can take a few months to arrive.

How to earn more per book

  • Choose a smaller page count or a larger trim size to reduce printing cost.
  • Price above the sweet spot where your rate and margin are strongest, and test small price changes.
  • Use black & white interiors unless color is essential.
  • Plan your page count first with the Word Count to Pages Converter.
  • Offer both a paperback and an ebook, since many readers prefer each format.

Frequently asked questions

How much does KDP pay per paperback sold?

For Amazon sales, a percentage of the list price (up to 60%) minus the printing cost of that copy. Expanded Distribution pays a lower rate.

Can a paperback lose money?

KDP will not let you price below the minimum that covers printing, so the royalty itself stays above zero. But advertising and upfront costs can still make a book unprofitable.

Are these exact figures?

No. Rates, printing costs and fees change by marketplace and over time. Always confirm the numbers in your KDP account before you set a price.

Important: This article is general information and not financial advice. Examples use US figures from KDP's published formulas and may differ in your marketplace. Check Amazon KDP's current royalty and printing cost pages before publishing.
