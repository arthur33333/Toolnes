Book Launch Tips

  1. Proofread the manuscript and check the final interior file.
  2. Verify that the cover matches the selected book format and specifications.
  3. Review the title, subtitle, author name, description and pricing.
  4. Check the relevant rights, permissions and content requirements.
  5. Preview your files and fix any issues before submitting.
  6. Prepare promotional content and a realistic launch plan.
  7. After publication, verify the live listing and review performance.
