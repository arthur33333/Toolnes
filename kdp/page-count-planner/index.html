How to use this planner

  1. Select a template or keep Custom book.
  2. Enter the estimated number of pages for each section.
  3. Choose whether your layout needs an even total or a multiple of four.
  4. Calculate the estimate, then copy or download your report.

For the most useful estimate, use page counts from your formatted interior file rather than estimating from the number of words alone.

Frequently asked questions

Does word count equal page count?

No. Page count depends on trim size, font, font size, margins, line spacing, paragraph spacing, illustrations, and other layout choices. Format your manuscript to get a more reliable count.

Should a book have an even number of pages?

Printed interiors are laid out as facing pages, and the final file may need blank pages depending on its structure. Use the adjustment option only when appropriate for your chosen format.

Is this an official Amazon KDP calculator?

No. Toolnes provides an independent planning estimate. It does not submit files to KDP or guarantee that a manuscript meets current publishing requirements.

Does this calculate book spine width?

No. Spine width depends on the final page count, paper type, and printing specifications. Use the separate Spine Width Calculator after your interior page count is finalized.
