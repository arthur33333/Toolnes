How to Use This KDP Royalty Calculator

  1. Choose a currency for your estimates.
  2. Enter your book's list price.
  3. Select an example royalty rate or enter a custom rate.
  4. Enter the printing cost per copy displayed for your book.
  5. Set your expected sales and any upfront publishing costs.
  6. Review your estimated royalties and break-even target.

The calculator uses the royalty rate you select and subtracts the printing cost you enter. It is designed for planning, not as a replacement for your official KDP royalty report.

Important: These are simplified estimates, not guaranteed Amazon KDP payouts. Actual royalties depend on the marketplace, book format, pricing eligibility, distribution option, printing charges, delivery fees, taxes and current KDP rules. The selected royalty percentage is an input, not a confirmation that your book qualifies for that rate. Check Amazon KDP's current official pricing and royalty information before publishing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I earn per book?

Your estimated earnings depend on your list price, applicable royalty rate and production costs. Change the inputs above to compare possible outcomes.

What is break-even sales?

It is the estimated number of copies you need to sell for your royalties to cover the upfront costs you entered. If your estimated royalty per copy is zero or negative, break-even cannot be reached under these assumptions.

Are these official Amazon KDP calculations?

No. This is an independent planning tool. Use the current Amazon KDP calculator and your account's reports to verify actual printing costs, eligibility and royalties.
