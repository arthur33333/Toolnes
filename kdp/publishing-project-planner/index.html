How to Plan a Self-Publishing Project

  1. Define your book format, budget and target launch date.
  2. Complete your manuscript and revise the content.
  3. Prepare the interior layout and book cover.
  4. Review metadata, keywords, categories and pricing.
  5. Check the final files and upload them to your chosen platform.
  6. Review the preview, resolve issues and plan your launch.

Timelines vary by project. Leave room for revisions, proof copies and platform review before announcing a firm launch date.
