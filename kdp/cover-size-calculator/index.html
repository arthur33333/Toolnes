How KDP Cover Size Is Calculated

KDP paperback covers are one single file that wraps the back cover, spine and front cover. The formulas are:

Cover width = bleed + back cover + spine + front cover + bleed
Cover height = bleed + trim height + bleed
Spine width = page count × paper thickness factor

KDP requires 0.125 in (3.2 mm) of bleed. For example, a 6 × 9 in book with 120 black & white pages on white paper has a spine of about 0.270 in. The full cover is 12.52 in wide and 9.25 in tall.

Tips for your cover file

  • Extend your background art all the way to the bleed edge.
  • Keep important text well inside the trim line.
  • Design at 300 DPI so the print stays sharp.
  • Changing the page count changes the spine, so finalize your interior first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a KDP paperback cover size?

Add the back cover width, spine width and front cover width, plus 0.125 in of bleed on both outside edges. The height is the trim height plus 0.125 in of bleed at the top and bottom.

What is bleed on a book cover?

Bleed is extra artwork that extends past the trim edge so no white edge shows after cutting. KDP paperback covers need 0.125 in of bleed on all sides.

How is spine width calculated?

Multiply the page count by a paper thickness factor. The factor depends on the interior ink and paper type, which is why white, cream and color books have different spines.

What DPI should my cover be?

Design your cover at 300 DPI for sharp printing.
