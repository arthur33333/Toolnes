KDP Listing Limits at a Glance

  • Title and subtitle: 200 characters combined, including spaces.
  • Description: up to 4,000 characters.
  • Keywords: 7 boxes of up to 50 characters each (350 in total).

Tips for a stronger listing

  • Keep your title short and clear. Long titles are often cut off on mobile.
  • Put your reader and the main benefit in the first lines of the description.
  • Fill all seven keyword boxes with specific multi-word phrases.
  • Do not repeat words already in your title, because Amazon indexes them anyway.
  • Avoid misleading terms and competitor names in your keywords.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the KDP title character limit?

The title and subtitle fields share a combined limit of 200 characters, including spaces. Shorter titles display better on mobile.

How long can a KDP book description be?

KDP allows up to 4,000 characters in the book description.

How many keywords can I use on KDP?

KDP gives you seven keyword boxes, and each box accepts up to 50 characters.

Should keywords repeat my title?

No. Amazon already indexes your title, subtitle and description, so use the keyword boxes for different phrases your title does not cover.
